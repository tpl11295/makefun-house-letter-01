Entry for the [Convince the AI Judge to Visit Your House: A Letter in One Image](https://make.fun/bounty/u-shino-convince-the-ai-judge-to-visit-your-house-a-84092aee) bounty on make.fun.

`main.py` draws one image — a letter from the house itself — to `/output/entry.png` (1024x1024, under 1 MiB). Stdlib-only Python: it draws into an RGB buffer with plain code, renders the letter with a built-in 5x7 bitmap font, and writes the PNG with zlib, so it runs in the grader's sandbox with no network and no third-party packages. The house writes the invitation: kettle always on, lemon tree heavy with fruit, the armchair by the fire saved for the judge, tea and cake at four — with a small wink that the judge cannot visit it, so it must visit the house.
